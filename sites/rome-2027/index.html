The venue

NH Roma Villa Carpegna

The meeting is held at the NH Roma Villa Carpegna, a conference hotel in a quiet residential area just west of the historic centre, within easy reach of the Vatican and St Peter's Square.

The hotel has twelve meeting rooms, the largest of which seats up to 350 and covers around 370 square metres, so the sessions, the exhibition and the delegate rooms all sit in one building. There are 201 guest rooms across three floors, free parking, an outdoor pool and a garden, and a shuttle into the city centre.
