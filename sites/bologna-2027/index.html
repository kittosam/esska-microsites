Welcome and Introduction

A message from the chairs

The chairs

Organising committee

Dear Colleagues,

It is our pleasure to welcome you to the first ESSKA Sports Medicine Focus Meeting, dedicated to Return to Performance following ACL Reconstruction in Football Players, taking place on 26–27 June 2027 at the Isokinetic Campus in Bologna, Italy.

This meeting will bring together orthopaedic surgeons, sports medicine physicians and physiotherapists to explore the pathway from ACL injury to return to performance. Our aim is to connect surgical and rehabilitation perspectives through an open discussion of the decisions that shape a player’s recovery and return to the demands of football.

Over one and a half days, the programme will address injury mechanisms and associated lesions, surgical treatment and acute phase management, strength recovery and movement quality restoration. We will also explore neuroplasticity, the on-field rehabilitation continuum and how football clubs understand and assess return to performance.

Particular attention will be given to the consequences of failed return to performance and secondary injuries, including their economic impact. These discussions will consider professional football across divisions, recognising the importance of these issues for players and clubs beyond the highest level of the game.

We have designed this Focus Meeting around a small group format, with time for questions, close interaction with the faculty and exchange between colleagues from different disciplines. We warmly welcome residents and fellows alongside experienced clinicians, and encourage participants to bring their own questions and clinical experiences to the discussion.

The Isokinetic Campus, with its dedicated sports medicine and rehabilitation facilities, provides a setting closely connected to the everyday challenges of helping athletes return to performance. We look forward to sharing this environment with you and to making this first meeting a valuable opportunity to learn from one another.

We look forward to welcoming you to Bologna in June 2027.Warm regards,Francesco Della Villa and Stefano Zaffagnini
Chair and Co-Chair, with the Organising Committee, Lior Laver and Stefano Di Paolo

What the meeting covers

ACL reconstruction

ACL reconstruction

Day one · Topics 1 and 3

The ACL injury mechanism and associated lesions, then surgical treatment and management of the acute phase.

Return to performance

Return to performance

Days one and two · Topics 4 to 6

Strength management (time is muscle), movement quality restoration, and neuroplasticity and its implications for performance.

Football players

Football players

Days one and two · Topics 2, 7 and 8

The economic impact of a failed return to performance, the on-field rehabilitation continuum, and how the football club perceives return to performance.

From Bologna Airport (BLQ)

Take the Marconi Express monorail from the airport to Bologna Centrale railway station; trains run every few minutes and the ride takes about 7 minutes. The campus is on the western side of the city, so a taxi from the airport is also a short journey.
