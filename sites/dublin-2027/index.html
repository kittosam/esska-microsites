Welcome and Introduction

A message from the chairs

The chairs

Dear Colleagues,

We are delighted to welcome you to Getting It Right First Time: Mastering Primary ACL Reconstruction, Managing Rotational Laxity and Optimising Rehabilitation, the ESSKA Sports Knee Section Focus Meeting, in Dublin on 14–15 May 2027.

Successful primary ACL reconstruction begins with a thorough understanding of the patient and extends through surgical planning, technical execution and rehabilitation. This comprehensive two-day course brings these stages together in an interactive, practical programme, exploring the decisions and details that shape each patient’s treatment.

Live patient assessment workshops, interactive MRI interpretation and case-based discussions will guide participants through clinical examination, individualised surgical planning and graft selection. Throughout the meeting, we will encourage questions, discussion and the exchange of experience with expert faculty.

Four dedicated re-live surgery sessions lie at the heart of the programme, covering graft harvest and preparation; tunnel placement, graft passage and fixation; surgical pearls, pitfalls and performance; and lateral procedures for rotational laxity. Expert commentary will connect technical demonstrations with clinical decision-making, highlighting practical tips, common challenges and strategies to avoid complications.

The programme will also explore when ACL reconstruction alone may be sufficient and when additional lateral stabilisation should be considered. A practical rehabilitation workshop will bring surgeons and specialist physiotherapists together to address early recovery, from restoring extension and activating the quadriceps to managing swelling, gait and challenging rehabilitation cases.

Designed for orthopaedic surgeons, fellows, senior trainees, physiotherapists and the wider multidisciplinary ACL team, this meeting offers an opportunity to deepen knowledge, refine technique and strengthen collaboration across the patient journey.

We look forward to welcoming you to two days of shared learning and discussion, with one clear ambition: getting it right the first time.
Brian Devitt, Martyn Snow and Sven Scheffler

The course

How the course works

What the course covers

  • Live patient assessment and clinical examination workshops.
  • Interactive MRI interpretation and surgical decision-making algorithms.
  • Re-live surgery sessions with expert faculty commentary.
  • Comprehensive management of rotational laxity.
  • A practical rehabilitation workshop led by surgeons and specialist physiotherapists.

What you will take away

  • Assess the ACL-injured patient using a structured clinical approach.
  • Interpret MRI findings and integrate them into surgical decision-making.
  • Select the most appropriate graft for individual patients.
  • Optimise graft harvest, tunnel placement, graft passage and fixation.
  • Identify patients at risk of residual rotational laxity.
  • Understand the indications and techniques for lateral extra-articular procedures.
  • Manage common early rehabilitation challenges after ACL reconstruction.

For orthopaedic surgeons, fellows, senior trainees, physiotherapists and members of the multidisciplinary ACL team.
