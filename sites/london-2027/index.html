Welcome and Introduction

A message from the chairs

The chairs

Dear Colleagues,

We are delighted to welcome you to London for the ESSKA Foot and Ankle Section Focus Meeting 2027, dedicated to From Mechanism to Match Fitness: Managing Syndesmosis Injuries in Athletes.

This meeting will bring together colleagues with a shared interest in the care of athletes with syndesmosis injuries. The programme follows the clinical pathway from understanding the mechanism of injury and assessing ankle stability to choosing treatment and planning a return to sport.

We will begin with anatomy, biomechanics and the diagnostic pathway, before exploring the initial clinical assessment, classification and treatment decision-making. The programme will address non-surgical management and the rationale for surgery, including the IFASC consensus on HAS in elite athletes. Afternoon sessions will focus on stabilisation options, chronic syndesmosis injuries, postoperative care and return to sport.

Discussion is central to the day. Alongside the lectures, a round-table session on real-life cases and difficult decisions will provide an opportunity to examine how different approaches apply in practice. A sponsored re-live surgery session will offer a further setting for discussion with the faculty. We encourage you to bring your questions and share your own clinical experience.

We hope the meeting will help you reflect on the decisions you face in practice and exchange ideas with colleagues working across the athlete’s recovery pathway. There will also be time to reconnect and make new contacts during the breaks.

We look forward to welcoming you to London and to an engaging day of learning and discussion.
Mette Andersen and Pieter D’Hooghe

Programme modules

Five modules, one theme

Small-group cadaveric teaching on managing syndesmosis injuries in athletes, from evaluation and testing to repair of the syndesmosis, the deltoid and the lateral ligaments.

  1. 01

    Syndesmosis evaluation and testing

    Clinical exam, intra-operative assessment

  2. 02

    Syndesmosis repair and augmentation

    Anatomical repair, suture-tape reinforcement

  3. 03

    Suture button fixation

    Placement, tensioning, pitfalls

  4. 04

    Deltoid ligament repair

    Anchors, augmentation techniques

  5. 05

    Lateral ligament repair

    Broström, InternalBrace-style constructs
